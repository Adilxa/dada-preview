Store-ready, not yet listed

DaDa is in a closed beta through Apple TestFlight. It has not been published on the App Store or Google Play yet.

For a buyer this is an advantage: the app can go live in both stores under your company's developer account, with your name, brand and pricing from the first day. There is no existing listing, reviews or audience to migrate.

If you prefer, we can publish it first and transfer the listing to your account later. Apple and Google both support moving an app between developer accounts.
